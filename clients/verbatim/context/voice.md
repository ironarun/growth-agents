# Verbatim Voice Context

**State as of:** 2026-07-25

## Voice

Verbatim should sound:

- direct
- skeptical
- adult-to-adult
- slightly contrarian
- useful before clever

## Preferred language

Use language such as:

- second opinion
- suspiciously polished
- challenge the answer
- something felt off
- too smooth to trust
- confidence before you act
- what is missing
- what could go wrong

## Avoid

- leverage
- transform
- revolutionize
- AI-powered
- unlock
- seamless
- generic SaaS optimism
- claims of guaranteed truth or correctness

## Consumer versus technical language

For consumer acquisition, prefer simple language such as `second opinion`, `suspiciously polished`, and `challenge it`.

`Adversarial review` remains valid product positioning on professional or technical surfaces. The current campaign headline, `Adversarial Review for ChatGPT and Claude`, is an approved campaign element and must not be silently rewritten.

## Punctuation rule

Do not use em dashes in customer-facing copy.

Use periods, commas, colons, or rewrite the sentence.

## Approval rule

Approved campaign copy is durable state. A future agent may recommend variants, but it must not replace approved copy in the manifest without a recorded human decision.
