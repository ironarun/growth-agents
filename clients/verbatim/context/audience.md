# Verbatim Audience Context

**State as of:** 2026-07-25

## Current paid-acquisition audience

Heavy daily users of:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Grok
- Perplexity
- similar conversational AI tools

These users are not AI skeptics. They use AI frequently and already receive value from it.

Their problem is more specific: some answers look too smooth, too confident, or too finished to trust, but they have no simple action to take when that feeling appears.

## Current campaign insight

```text
Polish can be the tell.
```

The user recognizes the feeling before they know how to diagnose it. Verbatim turns that doubt into a second-opinion workflow.

Emotional payload:

```text
Vindication, not fear.
```

The desired reaction is:

```text
I knew that sounded off.
```

## Audience exclusions for this campaign

Do not frame Suspiciously Polished as a campaign for:

- consultants
- lawyers
- enterprise governance teams
- compliance buyers
- general AI skeptics
- people who rarely use AI

## Historical consultant test

The prior consultant campaign was paused after about $50 in spend, 47 outbound clicks, 14 landing-page views, and no AddToChrome events or new users.

That result means the tested combination failed:

```text
consultant audience
+ professional-risk angle
+ prior creative
+ prior landing page
+ prior offer
```

It does not prove that consultants can never become Verbatim customers, and it does not prove that Meta cannot work for Verbatim. Treat the consultant campaign as historical learning, not the current targeting default.
