# Verbatim Brand Context

**State as of:** 2026-07-25

## Brand position

Verbatim is a confidence layer for AI work.

It helps users pressure-test important AI answers before relying on them.

Sharper brand energy:

```text
A.I. JUST GOT A BULLSHIT METER.
```

Use that energy selectively. The brand should feel direct, skeptical, useful, and adult-to-adult.

## Visual identity

- Primary brand color: `#F12258`
- Light pink surface: `#FFF0F2`
- Use the official Verbatim logo only.
- Do not invent a substitute logo or redraw the mark from memory.
- When an approved creative only needs the correct logo, use a deterministic overlay instead of regenerating the entire image.
- Do not invent product UI when real product screenshots or approved product proof are available.

## Paid landing-page rules

- Prefer a logo-only header for a focused paid-acquisition page.
- Do not add full site navigation unless Arun explicitly approves it.
- Reviews or concrete proof should appear high on the page.
- Product behavior must be grounded in current product truth.
- Do not imply that highlighting starts Debate.
- Do not describe Library as the place where distrusted answers are sent.

## Creative rules

- The image must communicate the campaign idea before the viewer reads the body copy.
- The image CTA and Meta CTA must not make incompatible promises.
- Do not use `Free` unless the current product and offer explicitly support that claim.
- The approved Suspiciously Polished assets use `Add to Chrome`, not `Add to Chrome Â· Free`.
- Customer-facing copy must not use em dashes.
