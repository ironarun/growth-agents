# Verbatim Product Context

**State as of:** 2026-07-25

## Core product truth

Verbatim is a Chrome extension and confidence layer for AI work. It helps users challenge important AI answers before relying on them.

The product came from a repeated manual behavior:

1. Receive an important answer from one model.
2. Ask another model to critique it.
3. Identify what is missing, wrong, overstated, or under-examined.
4. Bring the critique back to improve the original answer.

## Feature model

### Debate

Debate is the skepticism layer and the current paid-acquisition wedge.

- Debate starts from the Verbatim V button.
- It challenges a specific AI answer or conclusion.
- It gives the user's doubt somewhere to go.
- Do not claim that highlighting starts Debate.

### Library

Library is the memory layer.

- Highlighting belongs to Library.
- Library preserves valuable AI outputs.
- We do not highlight what we distrust.
- We highlight what we want to keep.

### Council

Council compares multiple models in parallel.

It is for situations where the user wants several model views rather than one challenger.

### Insights

Insights tracks model behavior and performance patterns over time.

It helps the user see how models compare across their own work. It should not be framed as a universal public leaderboard.

## Current acquisition wedge

The current campaign leads with Debate through a consumer insight:

```text
Some AI answers look too smooth, too confident, or too finished to trust.
```

The action is a second opinion from another model.

## Product claims to avoid

- Do not call Verbatim a generic AI wrapper.
- Do not say Verbatim makes an answer correct.
- Do not say another model is inherently trustworthy.
- Do not say highlighting is how a user marks an answer as suspicious.
- Do not call the product free when it requires a paid subscription after the trial.
- Do not invent product flows, screenshots, metrics, or benchmarks.
