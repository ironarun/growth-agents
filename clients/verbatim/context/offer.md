# Verbatim Offer Context

**State as of:** 2026-07-25

## Offer

The user installs the Verbatim Chrome extension to challenge AI answers inside the tools they already use.

The commercial model is trial-to-paid subscription access.

## Public offer rules

- Verbatim is not a permanently free product.
- Do not write `Free Chrome extension`.
- Do not write `Add to Chrome Â· Free`.

- The approved Meta CTA is `Download`.

- Do not store exact pricing in campaign copy unless it has been verified from the current product source of truth.

## Current acquisition promise

```text
When an AI answer feels too polished or too confident, have a rival model challenge it before you act.
```

The promise is increased confidence through challenge. It is not guaranteed truth or guaranteed correctness.
